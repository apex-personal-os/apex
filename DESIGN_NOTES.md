# Design notes

The UI Definition controls visual and interaction decisions. The product specification controls calculations and behavior.

- Central CSS tokens supply the Mist and Dusk palettes, spacing, type, depth, corners and motion. Device preference is the initial choice.
- The sign-in page uses an abstract, drifting background as specified, with a static version under reduced motion.
- The foundation includes an empty workspace. Realistic demo content belongs to the B1 seed script.
- Visual review covers 390, 820 and 1440 px in both themes. Check keyboard interaction, touch targets, contrast and reduced motion alongside screenshots.
